Content Creator App

Content Creator App is a desktop application that runs on the creator's own computer. It takes recordings of the creator's own streams, finds the moments worth keeping, cuts them into short vertical clips, and schedules them for posting to the creator's own YouTube channel.

It connects to one YouTube account, the creator's own, to upload clips and to read the channel's performance data so that future clips can be chosen better.

The app is used privately by its author and is not distributed to the public.
