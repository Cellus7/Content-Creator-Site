Privacy Policy

Content Creator App is a desktop application that runs entirely on the user's own computer. This policy describes what data it accesses and how that data is handled.

What the app accesses

When the user connects a YouTube account, the app asks Google for permission to:

  • view the channel's videos and their details;
  • view YouTube Analytics reports for the channel;
  • upload videos and read caption tracks on the channel's own videos.

The app accesses only the YouTube channel that the user signs in with. It does not access any other Google account data.

How the data is used

Channel data is used for one purpose: to choose and post short clips on the user's own channel. Analytics data is used to learn which clips perform well so that later clips can be chosen better.

Where the data is stored

All data, including Google sign-in tokens, video metadata and analytics, is stored locally on the user's computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app's author. The app has no backend.

Clip footage is analysed by third-party AI services (Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude) under their respective terms. Only the user's own recordings are sent to those services, and no YouTube account data is shared with them.

Sharing and sale

The app does not share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone. It does not use Google user data for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time by disconnecting YouTube inside the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder removes every stored token and record.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
